Section 1

Controls

PICO-8 maps Z to 🅾️ and X to ❎. Gamepads use the matching face buttons.

Section 3

Candy, lives, and the HUD

Lives, top left. Up to 4 at a time. A poisoned hit lowers your maximum, so only a Sugar Rush can refill you to the new cap.

Candy, top right, with the count beside it.

Blinking ghost: you were just hit and are invulnerable for 2 seconds, and will respawn.

Section 4

Creatures and their powers

Creatures arrive in waves, and each creature has a unique attack. Avoid touching creatures and their projectiles, as doing so costs 1 life.

MonsterSpriteWhen hurtHPPointsIn formationDiving
Frank 3300 Throws his head along with farm equipment projecticles straight down. A diving headless Frank will attack aimlessly, wandering down the screen. If the diving Frank has not yet thrown his head, he will move towards you. Be careful: If he gets below you, he will throw his head with deadly accuracy!
Witch 2400 Throws a potion aimed at you: poison, control or trick (see Creature Projectiles). Fastest diver. Plunges straight down while weaving side to side.
Drac
4500 Turns into a bat before diving. Drac flies down the screen until in-line with the player, then dashes sideways toward you. Be careful: Don't sit still for too long!
UFO 20700 Each time the horde fires, a UFO in formation has a 50% chance to fire a 12-shot ring instead. Sinks slowly, firing an 8-shot ring every second, then speeds off the bottom of the screen.
Jack 13010000 The final boss! Cycles through four attack patterns. See The Boss.

A diving creature that leaves the bottom or sides of the screen is gone for good, with no points. Killing a diving creature (busted!) has an increased point value along with an increased candy drop chance.
